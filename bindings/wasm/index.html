ForeFire in the browser

Starting over the Ajaccio hinterland, Corsica — the site of the repository's own test case — but the map goes anywhere: drag, zoom, and click to place the ignition. The fuel map and the terrain are decoded from map tiles in this tab, and Rothermel's rate of spread reads both, so the fire runs uphill and stops at bare ground. The whole run is computed up front — a fraction of a second to a few seconds — and the timeline scrubs through it.
